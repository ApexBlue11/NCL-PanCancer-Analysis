# Supplementary tables

Supplementary tables as referred to by S-number in the manuscript.

`Supplementary_Tables_S1-S10.xlsx` contains S1 to S10 as separate sheets. S11 is distributed only as a compressed TSV because it is too large for a workbook.

| Table | File | Rows | Cols | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | `S1_cohorts_and_tissue_mapping.tsv` | 33 | 7 | TCGA study abbreviations, full names, tumour and normal sample counts, and TCGA-GTEx normal tissue mapping with match-quality annotation |
| **S2** | `S2_differential_expression.tsv` | 66 | 19 | NCL differential expression per cancer against both normal comparators, with Cliff's delta, Hedges' g, confidence intervals and FDR-adjusted p-values |
| **S3** | `S3_stage_association.tsv` | 17 | 17 | Association between NCL expression and pathological stage: per-stage sample sizes, Kruskal-Wallis and Jonckheere-Terpstra statistics, late-versus-early effect sizes |
| **S4** | `S4_survival.tsv` | 84 | 24 | Log-rank, univariate Cox and multivariable Cox results for overall, disease-specific and progression-free survival, with covariates used and proportional-hazards diagnostics |
| **S5** | `S5_immune_infiltration.tsv` | 3,910 | 12 | All cancer x cell type x algorithm immune infiltration tests, unadjusted and purity-adjusted, across seven deconvolution algorithms |
| **S6** | `S6_algorithm_concordance.tsv` | 330 | 9 | Cross-algorithm concordance per cancer and canonical cell type, including the number of algorithms resolving each cell type |
| **S7** | `S7_immune_checkpoints.tsv` | 495 | 15 | NCL correlations with 16 immune checkpoint and immunomodulatory genes, unadjusted and adjusted for tumour purity and for proliferation |
| **S8** | `S8_genomic_and_immune_scores.tsv` | 230 | 8 | NCL correlations with immune, stromal and microenvironment scores, tumour mutational burden, MANTIS MSI score, aneuploidy score and fraction of genome altered |
| **S9** | `S9_cptac_validation.tsv` | 10 | 19 | Independent CPTAC protein-level validation across nine cohorts, rank-sum and paired tests |
| **S10** | `S10_gsea_per_cancer.tsv.gz` | 32,491 | 13 | Per-cancer pre-ranked GSEA against Hallmark and Reactome collections |
| **S11** | `S11_ncl_gene_correlations.tsv.gz` | 58,581 | 33 | Within-cancer Spearman correlation of NCL against every gene; the GSEA ranking statistic |
